RAUK - playing together (Minecraft 1.21.1, Fabric)

UPDATING: take any old rauk-1.0.x.jar out of the mods folder and put rauk-1.0.5.jar in.
1.0.5 rebuilds how Rauk finds its way: it runs in curves instead of zigzags, keeps to its plan,
cuts you off when it can get where you're going first, and remembers which rooms you spend your
time in. A 1.0.4 world works, but a NEW world is better (the rotunda lift's cage is fixed in it).
(1.0.4 reshaped the hotel and added the puzzles: worlds from before 1.0.4 need a new world.)
Host and friend should both have the same version.

BOTH PLAYERS
1. Install Fabric Loader for Minecraft 1.21.1 (loader 0.19.5 or newer):
   https://fabricmc.net/use/installer/  -> Client -> Minecraft 1.21.1 -> Install.
2. Open your .minecraft/mods folder (Windows: %appdata%\.minecraft\mods) and put the jars in it:
   - Host (you):   everything in host-mods   (Rauk, Fabric API), plus e4mc for Fabric 1.21.1
                   (from modrinth.com/mod/e4mc, or the Modrinth App's own mod browser)
   - Friend:       everything in friend-mods (Rauk, Fabric API) - or unzip rauk-friend-mods.zip
3. Start Minecraft with the "fabric-loader-1.21.1" profile.
   Modrinth App instead: make an instance (Fabric, 1.21.1) and put the jars in THAT instance's
   mods folder (instance -> ... -> Open folder), not in .minecraft. If the Modrinth App says
   "missing field synced_options", that's the app itself: update it, or make a fresh instance.

HOST
1. Create a NEW world (Generate Structures ON, Allow Cheats ON so you can use the test commands).
   Older worlds have an older hotel; in one, /rauk hotel restore rebuilds it.
2. Esc -> Open to LAN -> Start LAN World.
   e4mc puts an address in chat, something like  word-word.xx.e4mc.link  - click it to copy.
3. Send that address to your friend. It changes every time you open to LAN.

FRIEND
Multiplayer -> Direct Connection -> paste the address -> Join.

FINDING THE HOTEL (host, with cheats)
  /locate structure rauk:raukvik_grand      where it is (1500-3500 blocks out)
  /rauk hotel tp front                      to its front steps (also: lobby, atrium, chute, dorm, 4)
  /tp <friend> <you>                        bring your friend along
The way in is the coal chute on the west side. It falls in behind the first one through;
whoever comes later just steps onto the rubble and goes down with it into the coal store.
Inside you are in adventure mode. If Rauk gets you, you wake in the staff dormitory (B1).

IT IS MEANT TO BE DARK: inside the hotel your brightness setting is ignored. With the power off
only candles and fires give light. There are flashlights to find, but their beam doesn't show yet,
so until you get the generator going, /rauk hotel power on (or night vision) lets you see.
Sounds: the hotel's own sounds play on the Ambient/Environment volume slider.

THE WAY OUT
Five puzzles and then the lockdown itself. Start at the reception desk in the round hall at the
front corner, and read what people left behind: it tells you the rest. Rauk hears you at them.

USEFUL TEST COMMANDS (host, or /op your friend)
  /rauk hotel power on|off      the lights and lifts (the generator on B2 is the real way)
  /rauk hotel items             one of each thing to carry (keys, crank, fuses, flashlight)
  /rauk hotel progress          how far you've got; progress set <what> on|off, progress reset
  /rauk debug                   shows what Rauk senses and plans
  /rauk hotel rauk random       a new Rauk somewhere random in the hotel
  /rauk hotel lift guest 3      send a lift
  /rauk hotel solutions         this world's puzzle answers
  /rauk heat                    the rooms Rauk knows you spend the most time in
  /rauk nav                     Rauk's map of the hotel (and /rauk nav route <x y z>: its way there)
Lifts: press the button by a gate to call the car; in the car, click the lever to go up,
sneak-click to go down. Light switches are by each room's door.
